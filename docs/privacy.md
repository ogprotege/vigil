# Privacy

The canonical privacy guide is [Privacy, deletion, and notifications](user-guide/privacy-deletion-notifications.md).

> Last reviewed: 2026-07-26

It covers:

- direct provider requests and browser boundaries;
- Keychain accessibility after the first device unlock;
- normalized App Group records and Apple-managed backups;
- notification previews, widgets, and the optional app lock;
- credential-free diagnostic exports;
- account removal, recovery reset, and prior exports; and
- the limits of deletion outside Vigil's current local stores.

For vulnerability reporting, read [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md). For engineering assets, trust boundaries, and accepted risks, read the [threat model](threat-model.md).
